Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Simple Tech Solution LLC ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Simple Tally Counter mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We build our tools to respect your privacy. Simple Tally Counter does not require account creation, registration, or submission of personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

  • Local App Data: All counter values, names, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your tally counts on external servers.
  • Automatically Collected Data (AdMob): The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain device identification details, IP addresses, coarse location data, device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), and app interaction analytics to serve and measure advertisements.

2. How Information is Used

Data collected via third-party advertising partners is used for:

  • Displaying relevant banner and interstitial advertisements within the App.
  • Analyzing ad performance and preventing fraudulent activity.
  • Ensuring proper technical delivery of ad impressions.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The App integrates with third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google AdMob. These third parties collect information under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Analytics: Google Privacy & Terms

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is a general audience utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective steps.

5. Data Security

Since your tally counter data is stored entirely on your local device storage, your data remains as secure as your personal physical device. We recommend protecting your device with standard security PINs or biometric access.

6. Your Choices & Permissions

  • Device Advertising ID Reset: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings (Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads / Advertising on Android).
  • Data Deletion: Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally saved counter records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of the App following any updates signifies acceptance of the revised terms.
