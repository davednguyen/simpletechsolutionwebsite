Overview & How Diabetes Tracker Works

Diabetes Tracker is built to offer a simple, frictionless, and secure way for users to record blood glucose readings, track post-meal sugar spikes, monitor fasting levels, and spot long-term glycemic trends.

Much like a standalone, local baby kick counter app used during pregnancy, our app operates primarily as an on-device digital notebook. We prioritize local processing so that your sensitive daily glucose logs remain strictly under your personal custody and control.

Stored Locally

Logs stay on your hardware by default.

No Account Needed

No mandatory sign-ups or personal profiles.

Easy PDF/CSV Export

Easily share reports with your endocrinologist.

2

Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. The app only collects information that you manually enter or enable:

A. On-Device Health & Blood Sugar Logs

  • Blood glucose concentrations (mg/dL or mmol/L).
  • Meal tags (e.g., Fasting, Before Breakfast, 1-Hour Post Meal, 2-Hour Post Meal, Bedtime).
  • Carbohydrate counts, insulin dosage entries, or medication notes.
  • Timestamps and custom notes (e.g., physical exercise, symptoms, dietary intake).

B. Application Preferences & Technical Data

  • User settings (e.g., target glucose ranges, alarm/testing reminders, unit preferences).
  • Anonymous crash logs or basic app performance telemetry (only if permitted by your OS settings).
What We DO NOT Collect: We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, phone number, government identifiers, or precise GPS location data.
3

How We Use Your Information

Because your health data is stored locally on your mobile device, it is processed on-device solely to power app functionalities:

  • Visual Charting & Analytics: Plotting your post-meal spikes and glycemic curves into daily, weekly, and monthly visual graphs.
  • Testing Reminders: Scheduling local notifications (e.g., "Check blood sugar 2 hours after lunch") directly through your operating system.
  • Report Generation: Formatting your readings into shareable PDF summaries or CSV spreadsheets for your medical appointments.

Pregnancy & Gestational Diabetes Privacy Note

Maternal Health Privacy Safeguards

Many of our users are pregnant women managing gestational diabetes (GD) to protect both their health and their baby's development. Similar to a baby kick counter app, we treat maternal health data with maximum privacy sensitivity.

We understand that tracking blood sugar alongside gestational weeks can feel personal and sensitive. Diabetes Tracker does not sell, trade, monetize, or transmit maternal health data or pregnancy status to third-party ad networks, insurance companies, or data brokers.

Medical Disclaimer & Non-HIPAA Notice

Important Medical Notice

Diabetes Tracker is a logging software utility only. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, dosage calculation, or treatment recommendations.

  • Not a Medical Device: The app is not certified as a regulated medical device or Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM). Always rely on calibrated medical hardware and clinical guidance.
  • Non-HIPAA Covered Entity: The developer of Diabetes Tracker is not a covered entity or business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Because data stays on your phone, HIPAA standards do not govern this software. You are responsible for maintaining your device's passcode security.
  • Consult Your Healthcare Team: Never alter insulin dosages, dietary plans, or medication schedules without consulting your physician, endocrinologist, or OB-GYN.

Data Storage, Security & Cloud Backups

Your log data resides in encrypted application storage on your iOS or Android device.

OS Cloud Backups (iCloud / Google Drive)

If you have automatic device backups enabled on your smartphone (e.g., Apple iCloud Backup or Google Cloud Backup), your app database may be included in your personal cloud storage. These backups are governed directly by Apple's or Google's respective privacy policies and account security settings.

Your Rights & Data Control

Because we do not store your health entries on external servers, you maintain total autonomy over your information:

Export Your Data

You can export your complete sugar logs as a CSV or PDF file at any time from Settings.

Instant Data Erasure

Tapping "Clear All Data" in settings permanently purges all log entries from your device instantly.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Diabetes Tracker is designed for general audiences, pregnant mothers managing gestational diabetes, and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has entered information into the app on a shared device, erasing the app or clearing local settings completely deletes that data.
