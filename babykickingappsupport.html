Privacy Policy

App: Baby Kick Counter | Publisher: SIMPLE TECH SOLUTION LLC
Effective Date: September 21, 2026 | Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SIMPLE TECH SOLUTION LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information for the Baby Kick Counter mobile application.

1. Information Collection & Storage

Baby Kick Counter is built around local, on-device storage:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the app.
  • Local Data Storage: All session logs, kick timestamps, and notes created within the app are saved on your local device storage (and, if you use the Home Screen widget, in a storage area shared only between the app and widget on your own device).
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell your personal or activity data, and we do not use it for any purpose other than operating the app's features described in this policy.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Unless you've removed ads (see Section 3), Baby Kick Counter shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, as required under applicable law (e.g. GDPR, UK ATT). Google and its advertising partners may process limited technical and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads; we do not control this processing directly, and it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can decline personalized ads from the consent prompt, and you can reset or limit ad tracking at any time in your device's Settings.

We do not otherwise integrate analytics SDKs, and we do not track your precise location.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Baby Kick Counter offers optional paid upgrades, purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store:

  • Remove Ads (one-time purchase): permanently removes ads from the app.
  • Pro (auto-renewing monthly subscription): removes ads and unlocks the ability to export and share your kick data as PDF reports, CSV spreadsheets, and text summaries.

All payment processing is handled by Apple — we never see or store your payment card details. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled; you can view, manage, or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your device. Purchase status (which product you own) is checked directly with Apple's servers each time the app launches, using Apple's StoreKit framework, so that your purchase works across your devices.

4. Face ID / Touch ID App Lock

Baby Kick Counter includes an optional app lock that you can turn on from the About screen. When enabled, the app asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before it can be opened. This authentication is handled entirely by iOS: the app never receives, sees, or stores your fingerprint, face data, or any other biometric information — it only receives a yes/no result from the operating system. This feature works fully offline and never transmits anything to us or to any third party.

5. Exporting & Sharing Your Data

From the Insights tab, you can generate a PDF report, a CSV spreadsheet, and/or a text summary of your own kick data — for example, to share with a partner or a healthcare provider. These files are created entirely on your device. When you choose to share them (for example, through Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or a file storage app), that data leaves the app through the destination you pick, and is subject to that destination app or service's own privacy practices. We do not receive a copy of anything you export or share.

6. Children's Privacy

Baby Kick Counter is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Ads shown in the app are not targeted using children's data.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Important Medical Note: Baby Kick Counter is designed strictly as a personal logging tool for general tracking purposes. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional healthcare advice, diagnosis, or monitoring. Always consult your obstetrician, midwife, or medical provider if you have any questions or concerns regarding fetal movement.

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app's features change. We'll update the "Last Updated" date above when we do, and significant changes will be reflected here before or alongside the app update that introduces them.
