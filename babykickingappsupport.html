❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start counting baby kicks?
Most healthcare providers recommend starting daily kick counts around week 28 of pregnancy (or week 26 for high-risk pregnancies). Always consult your OB-GYN or midwife for personalized advice.
How long should a session take?
A standard session tracks how long it takes to feel 10 distinct movements (kicks, rolls, or flutters). Usually, 10 movements occur within 30 to 60 minutes, though it can take up to 2 hours during quieter periods.
How do I export my kick logs?
Your history is saved locally on your device in the "History" tab. The upcoming PDF export feature will allow you to generate a shareable summary directly for your doctor visits.
Is my health data private?
Yes! All kick sessions and timestamps are stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit or sell your pregnancy tracking data to external servers.
